## **Layer 1: Personalization Layer:**

- Extract info from opponent’s rap: name, style, slang, theme
- Build a “diss profile” (like “uses brags,” “targets money,” etc.)
- Feed that into your prompt: “Write a rap diss to a rapper who flexes wealth and uses internal rhymes…”

**Outcome:** Feels *personal* and tailored, not generic GPT lines

Preprocessing Layer(Before the prompt)

This is where your *technical edge* shows.

- NLP pipeline: Extract rhyme schemes, insults, themes.
- Opponent fingerprint: Analyze repeated phrases, subject matter.
- Pass that into your prompt as structured context.

Now it’s not just GPT — it’s GPT *with tools*.

## **Layer 2: Tone control**

- Choose if the response should be aggressive, witty, sarcastic, etc.

## **Layer 3: Style Transfer or Rap Persona Layer**

Let the AI battle as **Tupac**, **Eminem**, or even **Shakespeare.**

**How:**

- Use pre-designed prompt personas or fine-tuned styles.
- Example prompt: “Respond as if you are Kendrick Lamar, respond with a calm, spiritual diss.”

Outcome: It’s not just GPT — it’s *a character* spitting bars.

## Layer 4: FallBack:

If response generated by llm dont rhyme. How do we gurantee correct output everytime.

### **Option 1a:  Prime/Wrap the Prompt to Lock in Rhyme Structure**

Tell it *exactly* how to rhyme:

> Prompt trick:
> 
> 
> “Write a rap battle response with 8 lines. Use an AABB rhyme scheme. Each line must end with a rhyme. Match the tone of the opponent’s rap. Keep it aggressive but clever.”
> 

You can also say:

- “Use multisyllabic rhymes.”
- “Keep 8 syllables per line.”
- “Rhyme the last word of every pair of lines.”

LLMs need that level of instruction or they’ll get lazy.

---

### **Option 1b:  Use Few-Shot Prompting with Rhyming Examples**

Give it 1–2 perfect rap battles as examples.

Then say: “Now write your own.”

That way it mimics style, flow, and rhyme.

### SubLayer 2:  **Add a Postprocessing Rhyme Checker**

After GPT spits bars, check if they rhyme:

- Use a Python rhyme detection lib like `pronouncing`, `pyphen`, or `rhyme`:
    
    ```python
    python
    CopyEdit
    import pronouncing
    def do_they_rhyme(word1, word2):
        return bool(set(pronouncing.rhymes(word1)) & set([word2]))
    
    ```
    
- If rhyme fails, auto-regenerate that line or rerun GPT.

> 🔄 Fallback loop:
> 
> 
> “If rhyme fails, re-prompt with: 'Regenerate using proper AABB rhyming. Keep lines short and punchy.'”
